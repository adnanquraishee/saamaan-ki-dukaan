# Seller Policy & Commercial Terms — Kartly Marketplace

**Applies to:** Anvaya Retail Pvt. Ltd. (seller account) · **Version:** 2026.2

## 1. Commission structure
Kartly Marketplace charges a referral commission on the item price (inclusive of GST) of every delivered order, plus a fixed closing fee of ₹12 per order.

| Category | Commission |
|---|---|
| Apparel & fashion | 18% |
| Electronics & accessories | 8% |
| Home & kitchen | 14% |
| Beauty & personal care | 12% |

No other deduction may be applied to a settlement unless itemised under sections 3–5 of this policy.

## 2. Settlement terms
Settlements are paid on a T+14 day cycle from the delivery date of each order. Each settlement notice itemises gross order value, commission, closing fees, shipping recoveries, and any return or penalty deductions. Orders that are returned within the return window are reversed in the next settlement.

## 3. Return window
Customers may return eligible items within 10 days of delivery. For returns, commission is refunded to the seller less a reverse closing fee equal to the fixed closing fee.

## 4. Seller protection
If a returned item is received damaged, used, or different from what was shipped, the seller may raise a Seller Protection claim within 7 days of return receipt, with unboxing evidence. Approved claims are reimbursed up to the item's selling price.

## 5. Penalties
Late dispatch (beyond the promised handover time) incurs a penalty of ₹20 per order. Seller-side cancellations above 2% of orders in a week incur a ₹50 per-cancellation penalty.

## 6. Disputes
The seller may dispute any settlement deduction within 30 days of the settlement date by raising a ticket citing the order IDs and the clause of this policy that the deduction contradicts. Kartly Marketplace will respond within 7 business days. Undisputed deductions are final after 30 days.
